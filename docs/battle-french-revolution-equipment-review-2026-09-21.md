# French Revolutionary and early Imperial infantry review

Reviewed 21 September 2026. Scope: one original equipment-family GLB, two strict identity/date profiles, and equipment-only proposals for retained CDB90 French forces. The root integration applied all thirteen proposed event assignments and rebuilt the public catalogue.

## Equipment evidence and inspected images

- [Musée Carnavalet, Lesueur D.9065, _Joyeux départ des volontaires aux armées_, 1792–1793](https://www.parismuseescollections.paris.fr/fr/musee-carnavalet/oeuvres/joyeux-depart-des-volontaires-aux-armees). Contemporary gouache: blue coats, white lapels and crossbelts, red facings, cocked hats and long trousers. The complete scene and both details were inspected from the museum IIIF manifest, https://apicollections.parismusees.paris.fr/iiif/320039019/manifest. Local inspection files: `/tmp/historyofatlas-french-volunteers-0.jpg`, `-1.jpg`, `-2.jpg`. Hats and clothing vary; one raised hat shows three-cornered cocking. This supports a broad cocked-hat component, not one exact bicorne shape or an army-wide uniform. The model uses plain trousers in place of the depicted striped variants and omits unit-specific plumes and insignia.
- [Musée de l’Armée, infantry equipment article](https://actualites.musee-armee.fr/evenements/le-campement-de-la-grande-armee-episode-1-sequiper/). The first artifact in its collection plate is the 8th Infantry coat dated 1800–1805; the article places the change from cocked hat to shako in 1806. Inspected `/tmp/historyofatlas-french1800-habit.jpg`, downloaded from https://actualites.musee-armee.fr/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/MA_Collections-Grande-Armee.jpg. Later shakos, bearskin and 1812 coat on the same plate were not used to reconstruct the earlier figure. A later Rousselot drawing was inspected but excluded as an equipment source for this model.
- [Musée de l’Armée, Model 1777 flintlock, M 459](https://www.musee-armee.fr/fileadmin/user_upload/Documents/Support-Visite-Fiches-Objets/Fiches-Louis-XIV-Napo-Bonaparte/MA_fusil-1777.pdf). The object sheet places the type in Revolutionary and Imperial service. Page 1 image and measurements inspected: 1.52 m long, about 1.92 m with socket bayonet. Local PDF `/tmp/historyofatlas-fusil1777.pdf`, rendered page `/tmp/historyofatlas-fusil1777-page1.png`. Geometry distinguishes flintlock ignition from percussion, Martini breech and Mauser bolt systems; the loading animation is not reconstructed.
- [Archives parlementaires, decree of 7 September 1793](https://www.persee.fr/doc/arcpa_0000-0000_1908_num_73_1_46349_t1_0495_0000_4). This separate prescription includes a helmet for light infantry. It is an exclusion/caution against treating the new component as all French infantry.

## Registry and artifact

`french-revolution-infantry`: Q58296, 1792–1803. `french-imperial-cocked-hat`: Q71084, 1804–1805. Both share `/models/battles/french-revolution-infantry.glb`. Explicit and automatic dates are identical within each profile; an early Empire identity cannot select Republican equipment in 1796. No generic resolver option was introduced. Local armies require an explicit profile plus separately sourced equipment affiliation. Valmy is excluded because 20 September 1792 precedes the Republic and follows the king’s suspension; this review does not manufacture an affiliation to improve coverage.

Original authored geometry, not extracted museum imagery: 306,532 bytes, 7,984 triangles, 15 meshes, 32 nodes, March and Engage. Studio image `/tmp/historyofatlas-french-revolution-infantry.png` inspected. The visible family is a wide cocked felt hat, coat with lapels and tails, plain trousers and long flintlock/socket bayonet. Colour and small dress details are schematic. Catalogue total 18,935,492 bytes, under the unchanged 19,000,000 cap. Only the new GLB was generated with `--only french-revolution-infantry`; existing assets were not regenerated or simplified. Before the new production build, root compared all 56 previous exported GLBs under `out/models/battles` with their public counterparts: every file was byte-for-byte identical. Only the new French GLB was added.

## Validation and coverage

Baseline: published readiness `2026-09-21T19:55:40.173Z`. Actual `buildBattleSimulation` + `resolveUnitProfile` selected the new family for 84 formerly unknown directly identified formations (80 Republican, four Imperial): 72 scenes unknown → partial, 12 unknown → complete. Applying the 13 staged CDB assignments produces thirteen additional unknown → partial scenes. All 97 gains concern the specified formations; none proves a complete historical army or supplies absent strengths. The full rebuilt catalogue may also include independent data changes and must be measured separately.

The proposal passes `BattleEquipmentFileSchema`. A dry run confirms every explicit profile resolves, numerical fields and stable participant identities remain unchanged, and Valmy remains `unclassified-unit`. Artifact and rule tests pass: 30 tests across battle-units, battle-equipment and battle-equipment-identity. TypeScript typecheck, targeted ESLint and Python syntax compilation also pass. Browser verification is parent-owned after public/export rebuild.

Artifacts: `/tmp/historyofatlas-french-revolution-equipment-proposal.json`, `/tmp/historyofatlas-french-revolution-equipment-validation.json`, `/tmp/historyofatlas-verify-french-revolution.mts`. Read-only candidate inventory from mapping review: `/tmp/historyofatlas-french-revolution-cdb-candidates.json` (14 candidates; Valmy excluded).

Final runtime measurement: the family resolves in 98 dated records, of which 97 are mappable (92 Republican and five Imperial). The additional Imperial record Q4090309 still has no usable location; its equipment classification does not make it mappable. The full catalogue now has 57 GLBs and 62 profiles.
