# Detailed 3D battles on HistoryOfAtlas

User scope: the entire battle catalogue, optional animated armies at their historical locations, equipment appropriate to documented civilizations and technology, proportional forces and casualties. The user selected detailed 3D models.

## Implementation

1. Publish a dedicated versioned battle index and individual sourced records for every ingested battle, siege and naval engagement. Acquire previously skipped source candidates and account for missing dates/coordinates in a coverage report. Do not invent missing locations or dates.
2. Preserve quantitative statements, intervals, scope and references. Count participants, soldiers, ships and aircraft separately. Enrich reviewed battles with per-army documentary evidence. People listed as participants are not armies; unspecified coalitions remain unspecified.
3. Author articulated, material-rich GLB soldiers, cavalry, vehicles and vessels with dated equipment profiles. Assign culture only through reviewed participant/date rules or documented battle overrides; clearly identify representative equipment.
4. Add a lazy native MapLibre/Three custom layer, sharing the map canvas and depth. Geographic anchors work on globe and Mercator. Focused scenes retain a fixed model scale so users can zoom in to inspect equipment. Bounded scene/model budgets preserve map responsiveness.
5. Give both opposing forces one disclosed count-per-model scale. Unknown counts are illustrative groups, not numerical estimates. Use only sourced casualty proportions, distinguish deaths from casualties, and avoid double counting. Movement/formation geometry is illustrative unless separately documented.
6. Add a six-language battle catalogue and detail controls: select, play/pause, replay, seek, speed, quantities, sources, and focus. Keep reconstruction playback separate from historical chronology and pause competing clocks. Reduced-motion/shared links start paused.
7. Verify schema and quantity invariants, date gates, ratio allocation, URL/playback rules, the whole catalogue, browser rendering, model loading, location/projection correctness, animation frames, pause/seek, mobile, languages, failures and clean teardown. Build and check ordinary atlas regressions before deployment.

## Ownership

- Data agent: schemas, pipeline, acquisition/coverage and published battle files.
- Model agent: authored GLBs, reproducible generator, sourced equipment registry.
- Renderer agent: formation logic, native map layer and WorldMap lifecycle.
- Root: store/navigation, lazy catalogue client, localized UI, integration and verification/deployment.

## Acceptance limits to make visible

"All" means every reviewed ingested source record, with source coverage published. Undocumented battles cannot be certified. Missing forces/losses must stay unknown; source totals must not be split across camps without evidence. Shared source place coordinates are distinct from surveyed battlefield positions. Model materials and movements are an illustration, not a claim to reproduce exact uniforms or tactical maneuvers.
