# Land equipment source review · 21 September 2026

This review adds three authored equipment families and three automatic analogy rules for four identified sides. Selection still requires the recorded army identity and an eligible date; location alone supplies neither. A miniature represents a documented component, without establishing its share of an army, an exact uniform, or a historical drill movement. Strength and loss evidence are unchanged.

## Yarmouk remains partial

At Yarmouk (`Q194226`, 636), the reviewed Byzantine force explicitly selects `byzantine-spearman` as a period analogy. This does not reconstruct its allied contingents or exact equipment distribution. Rashidun equipment remains unclassified: [al-Baladhuri’s narrative at Fordham](https://sourcebooks.web.fordham.edu/source/yarmuk.asp) mentions swords but does not establish a complete equipment silhouette. Its differing numerical claims were not substituted for the existing reviewed estimates.

## Six CDB90 event amendments

These eight explicit assignments preserve the imported local army IDs, camp labels and numerical provenance. They do not replace aggregate armies with modern polities.

| Event                           | Reviewed component                                                              |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Edgehill `Q1478525`, 1642       | Parliamentary army → `civil-war-pikeman`                                        |
| Second Newbury `Q2031282`, 1644 | Parliamentary army → `civil-war-pikeman`                                        |
| Naseby `Q949724`, 1645          | Parliamentary army → `civil-war-pikeman`                                        |
| Ligny `Q855429`, 1815           | French and Prussian armies → `napoleonic-infantry`                              |
| Quatre Bras `Q705936`, 1815     | French army → `napoleonic-infantry`; British-Dutch aggregate remains unassigned |
| Cross Keys `Q1970207`, 1862     | US Army of West Virginia and Confederate Valley Army → `musket-infantry`        |

The parliamentary choices use the museum objects above. The [National Army Museum’s Waterloo campaign account](https://www.nam.ac.uk/explore/battle-waterloo) establishes the French and Prussian forces at Ligny and the separate French attack at Quatre Bras. The [National Park Service’s Shenandoah weapons survey](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/civil-war-weapons-in-the-shenandoah-valley.htm), based on written and archaeological evidence, supports the Cross Keys shoulder-arm family while emphasizing variation between weapons. Neither source determines the depicted army’s infantry, cavalry or artillery shares.

Marston Moor was omitted because its preserved CDB90 force name and actor label conflict. Ulundi was omitted from this amendment because `zulu-spearman` currently requires a polity ID and rejects the correctly preserved local CDB90 unit ID. No identity was changed to bypass that restriction.

## Verification and coverage limits

The three new GLBs total 1,071,392 bytes; each is below 400 KB. At the completion of that three-model batch, the catalogue contained 54 GLBs across 58 profiles, totaling 17,863,300 bytes. The later 1879 addition is documented in `battle-units.md`. Models load per scene. Automated checks cover identity/date/medium boundaries, opaque geometry, articulated bounds, full-length pikes, forward engagement and separate marching poses. Studio previews of all three families and pike engagement were inspected. The equipment amendment validator confirmed unchanged identities, camps, strengths and losses.

Before the CDB90 integration, a dry audit resolved 266 previously unclassified identified formations: 90 Byzantine, 109 Hispanic Monarchy and 67 Cavalier formations. Its 9,586 anonymous formations were unchanged. These are selection counts from that snapshot, not current global readiness totals or reconstructed troop populations; the rebuilt readiness report is authoritative for current coverage.

Caudebec (`Q24971262`, 1592) provides a browser check for `/models/battles/tercio-pikeman.glb`: the current record includes the Hispanic Monarchy as an identified polity within the window. Other unsupported participants retain their existing uncertainty. Browser results are recorded separately by the integration run.
