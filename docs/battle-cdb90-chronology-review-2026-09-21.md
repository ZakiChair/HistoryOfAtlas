## 26 — Q510943

Keep chronology hold. Cromwell's 20 August 1648 letter describes pursuit on 18 August followed by combat at Winwick the next morning, confirmed as 19 August by Historic England. Preston narrowly construed is 17 August; the larger battle-and-rout reaches 19 August. Neither scope independently establishes 18 August as the complete event end. The catalogue is not shortened to fit CDB.

- [Oliver Cromwell to William Lenthall, Warrington, 20 August 1648; Carlyle Letter LXIV](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Works_of_Thomas_Carlyle/Volume_6/Letter_64): Primary letter: marches through Wigan on the 18th, then fights at Winwick on the following morning; capitulation at Warrington follows.
- [Historic England registered battlefield 1412878, Winwick](https://historicengland.org.uk/listing/the-list/list-entry/1412878): Institutional interpretation identifies Winwick as 19 August 1648 and local Preston battle as 17 August.

## 249 — Q1445532

Keep chronology hold. Bulgarian museum dates 15–19 October 1912 are compatible with 28 October–1 November Old/New Style conversion, but the Turkish defence ministry chronology explicitly includes a Lüleburgaz battle entry on 2 November and documents competing chronologies. No primary evidence establishes that all relevant combat ended 1 November. An uncertainty endpoint is not substituted for the source active day, and the catalogue end is retained pending scope resolution.

- [Turkish Ministry of National Defence, Balkan Harbi Kronolojisi, printed page 46](https://www.msb.gov.tr/Content/Upload/Docs/askeritariharsiv/balkan_harbi_kronoloji.pdf): Official retrospective chronology places a Lüleburgaz battle entry under 2 November 1912 and cites differing chronologies in footnote 35; inspected through indexed PDF text because direct fetch returned 403.
- [Bulgarian National Military History Museum, October military calendar](https://militarymuseum.bg/voenna-istoria/voenen-kalendar/oktomvri/): Institutional regimental narrative lists Lüleburgaz–Bunarhisar as 15–19 October 1912; if Old Style, this corresponds to 28 October–1 November Gregorian; it does not independently exclude all fighting on 2 November.

## 294 — Q684872

Keep chronology hold. Hindenburg dates closure of the Augustów encirclement to 21 February 1915, which does not exhaustively date the end of the broader Winter Battle. The German Historical Museum uses 7–25 February, while the catalogue uses 22 February. These event boundaries remain unresolved; 21 February is not promoted from a particular decisive phase into the full event end.

- [Paul von Hindenburg, Aus meinem Leben, 1920, printed pages 124–125](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/30695/pg30695-images.html): Primary command memoir dates closure of the Augustów encirclement to 21 February 1915; no explicit statement that every part of the operation ended that day.
- [Deutsches Historisches Museum, Winterschlacht in Masuren, Manfred Wichmann, 14 September 2014](https://www.dhm.de/lemo/kapitel/erster-weltkrieg/kriegsverlauf/winterschlacht-in-masuren-1915): Institutional overview titles the operation 7–25 February 1915, demonstrating unresolved event-boundary variation.

## 303 — Q1582511

Keep chronology hold. Nixon's 17 January 1916 official despatch (London Gazette 29576, p. 4659, paragraph 20) explicitly reports gunboat fighting 22–25 November 1915; paragraphs 13–14, p. 4658, place Townshend on the field 25 November before his night withdrawal. An infantry-centric 22–24 November convention does not prove the whole battle ended 24 November. Catalogue end 25 November is not shortened to fit CDB.

- [Sir John Nixon despatch 17 January 1916, London Gazette 29576, 10 May 1916, p. 4658, paragraphs 12–14](https://www.thegazette.co.uk/London/issue/29576/supplement/4658/data.pdf): Primary official despatch: wounded evacuated and enemy reinforcements observed 25 November; Townshend withdrew that night.
- [Sir John Nixon despatch 17 January 1916, London Gazette 29576, 10 May 1916, p. 4659, paragraph 20](https://www.thegazette.co.uk/London/issue/29576/supplement/4659/data.pdf): Primary official despatch explicitly dates the gunboats engagement against hostile artillery 22–25 November 1915.
