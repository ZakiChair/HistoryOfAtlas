## Follow-up: previously unlocated equipped events

Three reviewed anchors are now added through `battle-metadata.json`. Neither changes battle dates, forces or quantities.

- **Point Judith, Q4872070**: [MARAD’s Black Point incident record](https://vesselhistory.marad.dot.gov/ShipHistory/Detail/6993) directly publishes 41°19′02″ N, 71°25′01″ W for the torpedoing of 5 May 1945. Arithmetic conversion gives **[-71.416944, 41.317222]**. This is the opening attack, not the later U-853 wreck site. [Tollaksen’s USNI account](https://www.usni.org/magazines/proceedings/1960/december/last-chapter-u-853) traces the resulting search southward. The source’s “Historic 1” accuracy label has no inspected metric radius; none is invented. Independent review confirmed both distinction and conversion. The USGS/NOAA survey PDF could not be retrieved (403), so it was not used.
- **Michelsberg, Q4090309**: the [Haus der Bayerischen Geschichte](https://hdbg.eu/koenigreich/index.php/objekte/index/id/277) establishes the hill assault on 15 October 1805. [Ulm/Neu-Ulm Touristik](https://tourismus.ulm.de/de/entdecken/sehen-und-erleben/sehenswuerdigkeiten/bundesfestung/wilhelmsburg-ulm) places the later Wilhelmsburg on the summit and publishes its map center (`48.4112119, 9.9830469`). The atlas uses **[9.983, 48.411]** only as an approximate hill-sector marker. The fortress dates from 1842–1849 and is not presented as an 1805 combat position. Three-decimal rounding establishes display resolution, not historical accuracy. The [Defence catalogue](https://bibliotheques-numeriques.defense.gouv.fr/musee-national-de-la-marine/document/09f848d1-b284-485b-b26a-22b059070c0b) corroborates the engagement’s name; its on-site Pelet map was not inspected.

**Sideling Hill, Q4872374 stays unlocated.** The [American Battlefield Trust](https://www.battlefields.org/learn/french-indian-war/battles/sideling-hill) explicitly says the exact location is unknown. Its narrative and fact panel also differ on force and casualty figures, so this pass adds no quantitative profile.

**Cape St. George, Q700530**: the official ONI/NHHC [2019 combat narrative](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVPUB-D221-PURL-gpo172671/pdf/GOVPUB-D221-PURL-gpo172671.pdf), printed p. 51 / PDF p. 65, contains a track-chart coordinate arrow. Two independent visual readings establish **5°16′20″ S, 153°44′30″ E**, converted to **[153.741667, -5.272222]**. The arrow points to DESDIV 45 at **0143**, beginning its approach toward the enemy; printed p. 55 corroborates that maneuver. It is not the first contact at 0141, an enemy position, a wreck, or the center of the later pursuit. No chart datum or navigation error is established. Contemporary claims about six Japanese vessels and four/five sinkings are superseded and were excluded from force/loss evidence. Source bytes, hash and inspected pages are recorded in `/tmp/historyofatlas-cape-st-george-review/manifest.json`.
