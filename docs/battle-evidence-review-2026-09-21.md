# Battle evidence review — 21 September 2026

Eight records were added to `data/curated/battle-profiles.json`, bringing the reviewed set to 22. These records supply opposing camps, comparable strengths and attributed loss evidence. They do not establish historical formations, reinforcement timing, troop composition or exact uniforms. Human losses and lost vessels retain separate units. All estimated quantities remain marked approximate.
