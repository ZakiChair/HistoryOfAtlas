# Data wave — 24 September 2026

This wave adds sourced content to the map in three streams. Every item was researched from pages fetched during the review and then re-verified by an independent reviewer who re-fetched the cited sources. Missing evidence was never turned into coordinates, dates, strengths, discoveries or adherence.

## Battles: 39 reviewed locations

The 300 best-known catalogue battles that had a date but no coordinates were examined. **39 were located** from heritage registers, gazetteers, academic sources and official site authorities, raising mappable battles from 16,860 to 16,899. **261 were refused**: operations, offensives, campaigns, invasions, blockades and wars that cannot honestly be reduced to one point; naval actions without a published position; disputed or regional sites. Refusals are kept in the review fragments; no refused record was changed.

## Resources: history before 1950

The new contribution `pipeline/resources/historical-world.json` adds **63 historical extraction sites** (gold and silver rushes, early oil fields, coal, copper, tin, diamonds, nitrates and salt) and **58 dated discovery or first-production attestations** on sites that already existed, instead of duplicating them. Sites known by 1800 rise from 25 to 62, by 1850 from 30 to 80 and by 1900 from 97 to 194. The verifiers removed seven items whose only evidence was an uncited encyclopaedia paragraph, whose source no longer resolved, or whose existing marker lay 16–29 km from the named workings.

## Religions: 39 milestones after 1600

`public/data/religions/history.json` gains **39 `spread` milestones** between 1600 and 1988 for the existing traditions (missions and churches in Africa, Korea, the Pacific and the Americas; Islam in West and East Africa and Southeast Asia; Jewish communities in the Americas; Hindu, Sikh, Jain and Zoroastrian diasporas; Buddhism and Daoism outside Asia; Shinto under Meiji; Yoruba-derived religions in the Americas) and **45 sources**. They record attested presence or establishment, never adherence. One milestone sourced only from a community website was removed by its verifier. See [the corpus description](religions-data.md).
