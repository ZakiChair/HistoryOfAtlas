## 4 — Battle of Dessau Bridge (Q677121)

Exact named battle on 25 April 1626 (15 April Julian in the contemporary broadside). Both complete source forces remain local. PROT UNION ARMY is an anachronistic source label: the Union dissolved in 1621. The original CDB total is attached to Mansfeld's field army, identified by source commander and contemporary narrative, not to a revived Union or a guessed polity.

The 1626 broadside identifies opposing complete armies and the decisive engagement on 15 April Julian / 25 April Gregorian. The later capture of Zerbst is narrated separately. Its account also gives 3,000 killed/captured from Mansfeld's infantry and around 1,000 Imperial battlefield losses, but this review does not independently certify CDB strength estimates.

## 11 — Battle of Wittstock (Q428972)

Wittstock municipality explicitly equates 4 October Gregorian with 24 September Julian 1636 and identifies the combined Imperial-Saxon army versus Baner and Leslie's Swedish army. Preserve the full coalition under a local ID. Modern force estimates differ from CDB; that alone does not establish a different event scope.

Whole battle on the matched day. Municipal estimates of 22,000 combined Imperial-Saxon troops and 16,000 Swedes differ from CDB 30,000 / 22,000; retain CDB as attributed estimates, without treating this identity review as confirmation of those magnitudes.

- [City of Wittstock and Museen Alte Bischofsburg: Battle anniversary, 2016](https://www.wittstock.de/news/1/353709/nachrichten/festwoche-zum-jahrestag-der-schlacht-bei-wittstock.html)

## 14 — Battle of Tuttlingen (Q1436705)

Chronology and casualty-scope hold. CDB and catalogue give 24 November 1643 only. ADB Mercy separates the attack on 24 November from Rantzau's surrender of 7,000 men on 25 November. CDB defender casualties are also 7,000, but their source attribution does not establish whether they exclude the next-day surrender. Do not promote a potentially multi-day complete loss into the one-day event. IMP ARMY would also require a local Bavarian-Imperial-Lorraine identity review.

The 24 November surprise and 25 November surrender are separately dated. NDB identifies Mercy's army with Lorraine and Imperial support. The matching 7,000 is a scope warning, not proof of CDB's exact derivation. Keep the hold pending its underlying loss source; no date override proposed.

- [Deutsche Biographie: Franz von Mercy, NDB and ADB biographies](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/sfz61891.html)

## 15 — Battle of Freiburg (Q174574)

Chronology hold despite matching CDB and catalogue 3–9 August 1644. Royal Collection RCIN 723051.a has a modern 3/5/9 summary but its transcribed contemporary 1644 title explicitly gives 3/5/10 August; ADB Mercy places the St Peter combat on 10 August. A later Versailles painting title uses 3–9. The source row includes a last active period on 9 August and total losses, so the final combat date and total scope need resolution before promotion. No source date is silently changed.

A contemporary printed date conflicts with the modern event range, within the Royal Collection record itself. CDB row 15 contains multiple periods for one battle, not duplicate independent battles. Keeping this unresolved row out does not assert that either chronology has been conclusively disproved.

- [Royal Collection Trust: contemporary Freiburg print, RCIN 723051.a, 1644](https://militarymaps.rct.uk/thirty-years-war-1618-48/view-of-the-battle-of-freiburg-1644-freiburg-freiburg-im-breisgau-baden-wurttemberg-germany)

## 17 — Battle of Herbsthausen (Q913821)

Historical date hold. CDB and catalogue agree mechanically on 2 May 1645, but the municipality of Bad Mergentheim and ADB Mercy explicitly date Herbsthausen to 5 May 1645. A three-day difference is not the Julian/Gregorian offset. A separate source-backed metadata correction changes the public date to 5 May; CDB retains 2 May and remains excluded.

The named battle and commanders fit, but the CDB day does not agree with independently documented historical dating. The metadata correction to 5 May leaves CDB 2 May incompatible; it is not a quantitative recovery.

- [City of Bad Mergentheim: History of Herbsthausen](https://www.bad-mergentheim.de/de/verwaltung/stadtteile/bad-mergentheimer-stadtteil-herbsthausen-id_436/)
- [Deutsche Biographie: Franz von Mercy, NDB and ADB biographies](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/sfz61891.html)

## 33 — Battle of Khotyn (1673) (Q3636391)

Full-battle temporal-scope hold, not rejection solely for divergent strength estimates. CDB and catalogue cover 11 November 1673 only. Wilanow museum describes artillery and an unsuccessful assault on 10 November before the decisive 11 November battle, and its account of a contemporary 1673 pamphlet also describes fighting and a contingent changing sides on 10 November. The row's 80,000 / 50,000 strengths and aggregate casualties are not demonstrably isolated to the 11 November phase. Keep the original estimates unchanged; no campaign total or guessed coalition allocation is substituted.

The museum explicitly separates other Ottoman corps away from Khotyn; it does not prove that CDB 80,000 includes them. The numerical discrepancy alone is not the hold. The unresolved issue is whole-battle totals against a one-day row when substantive combat and a change in force composition occurred the preceding day. No metadata override or quantity edit is proposed.

- [Museum of King Jan III Palace at Wilanow: Khotyn, 11 November 1673](https://wilanow-palac.pl/pasaz-wiedzy/chocim-11-xi-1673-o-slawe-i-korone)
- [Wilanow museum: account of contemporary 1673 Khotyn pamphlet held by the Gdansk PAN Library](https://wilanow-palac.pl/pasaz-wiedzy/prawica-pana-zeslala-mestwo-opis-przebiegu-bitwy-pod-chocimiem)
