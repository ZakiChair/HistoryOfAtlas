Content authored for this project is released under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
This covers, in particular:

- curated reviews and corrections in `data/curated/` and `docs/`;
- the religion milestones, descriptions, presence zones and diffusion links in
  `public/data/religions/history.json`;
- the map symbology, pictograms and emblems drawn by the project.

Attribute it as "HistoryOfAtlas contributors" with a link to
https://github.com/ZakiChair/HistoryOfAtlas.

Third-party data keeps its original licence. This includes Wikidata (CC0), Wikipedia
extracts (CC BY-SA), Cliopatria and Seshat boundaries, Natural Earth, CDB90, and the resource
inventories cited site by site (CC BY 4.0, NLOD 2.0, OGL-Canada, public domain, CC BY-NC-SA
and others). Each record names its source. The atlas's About page and
`public/data/licenses.json` list these sources and their licences.

3D models whose origin is not documented in their own metadata are not covered by this
licence until their provenance has been confirmed.
